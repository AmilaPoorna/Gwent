# How to install and play Gwent

Gwent is the card game from *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt*, with the original cards, board, sounds and music. You
play against the computer. It's free, and there's nothing to set up.

## Play on a PC (Windows, Mac or Linux)

1. Open **https://amilapoorna.github.io/Gwent/** in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
2. Click **Play**.

Press **F11** to play full screen.

**Optional: install it as an app.** In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar
(or open the browser menu and choose *Install Gwent*). Gwent then opens in its own window from your Start menu or
desktop.

## Play on an Android phone or tablet

### Install the app (recommended)

1. On your phone, open **https://github.com/AmilaPoorna/Gwent/releases/latest/download/gwent.apk**.
   The app downloads.
2. Tap the downloaded file (it is also in your *Downloads*).
3. If your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this
   source**, go back and tap **Install**.
4. If Google Play Protect warns that the app is from an unknown developer, tap **More details** and then
   **Install anyway**. The app is not from the Play Store, which is why the warning appears.
5. Open **Gwent** from your home screen. It runs full screen, in landscape.

Needs Android 7.0 or newer. If Gwent says it needs a newer web engine, update **Android System WebView** and
**Chrome** from the Play Store.

**Updating:** download *gwent.apk* again from the same link and install it over the old one. Your decks are kept.

### Or play in the browser

Open **https://amilapoorna.github.io/Gwent/** in Chrome and turn your phone sideways. To keep it on your home
screen, open the Chrome menu and choose **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

## How to play

The game has its own **How to Play** screen: on the home screen under **Play**, and in the menu during a match.

### The game in short

- A match is the best of three rounds. Each player starts with two gems and loses one for every round lost. Lose
  both and you lose the match.
- You start with 10 cards from your deck and may redraw up to 2 of them. You don't get new cards between rounds,
  so every card you play is one fewer for later rounds.
- Players take turns playing one card. Unit cards go on one of your three rows (close combat, ranged, siege).
  Their strengths add up, and the higher total wins the round.
- When you have played enough, **pass**. You can't play again that round, but your opponent can keep going.
  The round ends when both players have passed.
- Often the best move is to pass early in a round you can't win, and save your cards for the next one.

### Building your deck

After *Play* you see the deck builder. It is also where you choose what to play with.

- Pick a faction, then change your leader if you like.
- Add cards from **Card Collection** (left) to **Cards in Deck** (right). Tap or click a card in your deck to
  remove it.
- A deck needs at least **22 unit cards** and may have at most **10 special cards**.
- Your deck for every faction is saved automatically, so next time you only make changes.

### Factions

| Faction | Perk |
|---|---|
| Northern Realms | Draw a card from your deck whenever you win a round. |
| Nilfgaardian Empire | Win any round that ends in a draw. |
| Scoia'tael | Decide who takes the first turn. |
| Monsters | Keep one random unit (never a hero) on the board after each round. |
| Skellige | Two random units from your discard pile come back at the start of the third round. |

Every faction has several leaders. Each leader has one ability you can use once per match.

### Card abilities

| Ability | What it does |
|---|---|
| Agile | Can go in the close combat or the ranged row. |
| Medic | Play a unit from your discard pile (not heroes or special cards). |
| Morale Boost | +1 strength to every other unit in its row. |
| Muster | Brings all cards with the same name from your deck and your hand onto the board. |
| Spy | Goes on your opponent's side and counts for them, but you draw 2 cards. |
| Tight Bond | Units with the same name in the same row double each other's strength. |
| Scorch | Destroys the strongest unit(s) on the board. On some units it hits only one enemy row, and only if that row totals 10 or more. |
| Commander's Horn | Doubles the strength of all units in a row (one per row). |
| Hero | Not affected by weather, special cards or abilities. |
| Summon Avenger | When the card leaves the board, a stronger unit takes its place. |
| Berserker / Mardroeme | Mardroeme turns the Berserkers in its row into powerful bears. |

**Weather:** Biting Frost, Impenetrable Fog and Torrential Rain drop every unit in their row to strength 1, on
both sides. Skellige Storm hits the ranged and siege rows. Clear Weather removes all weather.
**Decoy:** swap it with one of your units on the board to take that card back into your hand.

### Controls

| What | PC | Phone or tablet |
|---|---|---|
| Add or remove a card | click | tap |
| Change faction | Q / E, or click the faction names at the top | tap the faction names at the top |
| Change leader | X, or click the leader | tap the leader |
| Look at a card up close | Z or right-click | press and hold the card |
| Start the match | Enter | tap **Start game** |
| Redraw a card (start of match) | click it | tap it |
| Play a card | click the card, then the row | tap the card, then the row |
| Pass | hold Space | hold **[HOLD] Pass** |
| Menu: How to Play, music, sound, restart, change deck, quit, forfeit | Esc | tap **Menu**, or the Android back button in the app |

Your decks and settings are saved on the device you play on. A different browser or phone starts with the
starter decks.
